# Privacy Policy

This page covers what data Zfolio collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

## Information you give us

- **Account info** — your name, and the email or phone number you sign in with, plus optional investor-profile fields you fill in (risk profile, index preference, age, investment amount).
- **Holdings** — stocks, gold, bonds and FDs you add manually, or that are synced from a broker you've connected.
- **Broker connections** — which broker you've linked, and its connection status. The broker access token itself is held only on our backend, never on your device, and is used solely to read your holdings on your behalf.
- **Support requests** — anything you send us via the in-app support form or email, including attached screenshots, so we can help.

## Information collected automatically

- Basic technical info (device type, app version, general region) needed to run and debug the service.
- Standard analytics and session-diagnostics tools, on both the website and in the app, to understand usage and catch bugs — see our [Cookies Policy](/legal/cookies/) for details and how to opt out.

## How your information is used

- To run your account and show you your own portfolio.
- To sync holdings from a broker you've connected.
- To calculate the portfolio analytics, performance comparisons, and optimization suggestions the app shows you.
- To respond to support requests.
- To send you transactional notifications (e.g. price alerts, rebalance suggestions you've opted into).

We do not sell your personal information.

## On-device storage

The app caches some data locally (holdings, prices, and reference data like this document) so it works offline and loads instantly. This cache lives on your device and is cleared when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

## Third-party data

Historical price data, fundamentals, and news are sourced from third-party data vendors and public feeds. This data is used to power the analytics shown to you and is not linked to your personal account information when sourced.

## Data retention and deletion

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time from **Settings → Privacy & Data Policy → Delete My Data** — this is a real, irreversible deletion, not a deactivation.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access-controlled backend storage) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we treat this seriously and continually review our practices.

## Children's privacy

Zfolio is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 18.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update the "last updated" date above whenever this policy changes. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.

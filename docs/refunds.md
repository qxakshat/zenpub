# Cancellation & Refunds Policy

## No refunds, in any case

**All payments made to Zfolio are final.** Once a subscription charge has been processed, **no refund will be issued under any circumstances** — including partial usage, dissatisfaction with the service, accidental purchase, early cancellation, or a change of mind. This applies to every plan and billing cycle Zfolio offers, with no exceptions beyond the billing-error correction described below.

This is the entire policy. The sections below explain what it means in practice — they don't soften it.

## Try before you pay

Every new subscriber gets a free trial period before any card is charged. Use it to decide whether Zfolio is right for you — you can cancel at any point during the trial and pay nothing. The no-refund policy only begins to apply once a real payment has actually been made.

## Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel anytime from **Settings → Subscription → Cancel** in the app. Cancelling:

- stops all future billing immediately — you will not be charged again;
- does **not** end your access early — you keep full access for the rest of the period you already paid for;
- does **not** entitle you to a refund for the unused portion of that period, in whole or pro-rata.

## Discounted pricing and referral rewards

Zfolio's discounted first-payment pricing and any free months credited to your account through the referral program are pricing and promotional mechanisms, not cash. They carry no refund or cash-equivalent value of their own, and are not affected by (or a basis for) any refund claim.

## Billing errors

The no-refund policy above covers subscription payments for service Zfolio actually provided. It does not cover a payment that was never supposed to happen at all — a duplicate charge, a charge for the wrong amount, or a charge that went through after you had already cancelled. If one of those happens, contact us at **zfolio.co.in@gmail.com** within 7 days with your payment reference, and we will correct the billing error. This is a correction of our mistake, not a discretionary refund, and doesn't change the no-refund policy for any charge that was correctly billed.

## How payments are handled

Subscription payments are processed through Razorpay. Zfolio does not store your card or bank details — Razorpay handles and secures all payment information directly.

## Questions

Reach out to **zfolio.co.in@gmail.com** for anything about billing, cancellation, or this policy.

# Cookies & Tracking Policy

This page covers how Zfolio's website and app use cookies and similar on-device storage.

## On the website (zfolio.co.in)

We use a small number of analytics and session-diagnostics tools, of the kind most websites use, to understand how visitors use the site and to catch bugs — anonymized usage patterns (pages visited, general traffic) and how visitors interact with the page.

None of this is used for advertising, and we don't run any third-party ad-tracking cookies. If you'd rather not be tracked, you can block these in your browser's cookie/privacy settings, or use a tracker-blocking extension — the site works fully without them.

## In the app

The app doesn't use browser cookies (there's no browser), but it does use two comparable mechanisms:

- **On-device storage** — caches your holdings, prices, reference data (including this document), and preferences locally, so the app works offline and loads instantly. This data stays on your device and is never used for cross-app tracking.
- **Usage and crash diagnostics** — helps us find and fix bugs.

## Managing these

- **Browser**: use your browser's cookie settings, or a tracker-blocking extension, to disable analytics on the website.
- **App**: clearing the app's storage (or uninstalling it) removes all locally cached data. Diagnostics can't be selectively disabled today, but they never include your personal account data — see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/) for what we do and don't collect.

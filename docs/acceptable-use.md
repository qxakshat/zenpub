# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy sets out what you may not do when using Zfolio. It applies alongside our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms/), and a violation of this policy is a violation of those Terms.

## You may not

- **Connect a broker account that isn't yours**, or that you're not authorized to access, or provide broker/API credentials belonging to someone else.
- **Access the Services by any automated means** — scraping, crawling, or scripted access — other than what a documented, authorized API explicitly allows, or in a way that ignores our `robots.txt`.
- **Reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble** the app or website, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits despite this restriction.
- **Resell, sublicense, or provide third-party access** to the Services, or to data or analytics obtained from them, without our written permission.
- **Circumvent subscription gates, usage limits, or paywalls** — including by creating multiple accounts to exceed free-tier limits, or by attempting to access premium features without an active subscription.
- **Upload anything harmful** through the holdings-upload feature or any other upload path — malicious files, or files that don't genuinely represent your own holdings.
- **Attempt to access another user's account or data**, or probe, scan, or test the security of the Services without our prior written authorization.
- **Interfere with or disrupt the Services** — including excessive automated requests, denial-of-service attempts, or anything that degrades the service for other users.
- **Use Zfolio to provide investment advice or recommendations to others** in a way that implies Zfolio's endorsement, or that would require a license (such as SEBI investment-adviser registration) you don't hold.
- **Misrepresent your identity**, or use the Services for any unlawful purpose, including violating applicable securities law.

## Reporting a violation

If you believe someone is violating this policy, email **support@zfolio.co.in**.

## Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and take action we consider appropriate, including warning, suspending, or permanently terminating an account, and — where required by law — reporting conduct to the relevant authorities. Enforcement action does not entitle you to a refund; see our [Refunds Policy](/legal/refunds/).
